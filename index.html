Track hours.
Own your flex.

FlexTally is the simplest way to track your work hours and stay on top of your flex balance. No subscription, no account, no nonsense.

  • 01Log start and end times each day with a smooth scroll picker — no typing, no fiddling.
  • 02Add breaks and tag days as WFH, Sick, Public Holiday, Flex Day, or Annual Leave.
  • 03See exactly where your flex balance stands across the week or fortnight.
  • 04Everything stays on your device. No cloud, no analytics, no tracking.

A small app for people who like their hours to add up.
