Common questions

Quick answers to the things people usually ask.

How much does it cost?

A$2.99 one-off purchase on the App Store. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads — pay once and it's yours.

Does it work without internet?

Completely. FlexTally never makes a network request. Everything lives on your phone.

How does flex tracking work?

You set your required hours per day. Time over that adds to your flex balance; time under subtracts. Take a flex day and it deducts a full day from the bank.

Can my week start on Thursday?

It can start any day you like. Same for fortnights — pick which week you're currently in.

Can I export my hours?

Yes — archive any pay period and share a CSV straight to email, Files, or anywhere else.

What if I get a new phone?

Restore from your iCloud device backup like any other iOS app and your data comes with you.
