FlexTally  /  Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 May 2026

The short version

FlexTally collects nothing, sends nothing, and shares nothing. Your data stays on your iPhone. The developer can't see any of it.

What this app does with your data

FlexTally is a personal time-tracking app that runs entirely on your device. Everything you enter — work hours, breaks, tags, comments, and settings — is stored locally on your iPhone using the operating system's standard local storage. It never leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. FlexTally does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on any server. There is no account to create, no login, no email address requested, and no usage telemetry. The developer has no access to anything you enter into the app.

Network access

FlexTally does not make any network requests. It does not connect to any server, including the developer's. The app behaves identically in airplane mode as it does online. You can verify this in your iPhone's Settings under FlexTally, where you'll see no toggles for cellular data or local network access, because the app requests neither.

Third-party services

FlexTally does not include any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services. There are no trackers of any kind.

Children's privacy

FlexTally is rated 4+ and is appropriate for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent regulations in other jurisdictions.

Deleting your data

To permanently delete everything FlexTally has stored, uninstall the app. There is no data held anywhere else that needs to be requested, exported, or erased. Once the app is gone from your device, the data is gone with it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL and the date at the top will be revised. Because the app collects no data, any future change would only narrow the policy further, not expand it.
